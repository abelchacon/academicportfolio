Machine learning for agricultural yield prediction: a case on sugar beet production in The Netherlands.

Research on the application of machine learning techniques for agricultural yield prediction, with a focus on sugar beet production in The Netherlands. The project explores the use of various machine learning algorithms to analyze historical yield data, weather patterns, and soil conditions to develop predictive models that can assist farmers in optimizing their crop management practices and improving yield outcomes.
