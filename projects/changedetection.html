TIMESERIES CHANGE DETECTION OF MEGADROUGHT AND HUMAN EXTRACTION IN “EL YESO” RESERVOIR, CHILE

Research on change detection in earth observation with a case study of the “El Yeso” reservoir in Chile, which has experienced significant water level decline due to a megadrought and human extraction. The project utilizes Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and machine learning techniques to analyze temporal changes in the reservoir's surface area, providing insights into the impacts of climate change and human activities on water resources.
