Suitability Cost Raster Analysis for optimal waste water routing in the Maas Waal region.

Research on the application of suitability cost raster analysis for optimal waste water routing in the Maas Waal region. The project utilizes ArcGIS tools and spatial analysis to evaluate the suitability of different routes for waste water management, considering factors such as topography, infrastructure, and environmental impact.
